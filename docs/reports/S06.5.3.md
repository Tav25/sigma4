# SIGMA4 S06.5.3: Smaller, crisp dimension type and proper planar drafting arrows

Date: 2026-11-03; branch: `sigma4`.
Base SHA: `db03d0deb23c3fdbb5d971f094cc5931a4f196da`.
Software version: **0.0.23**, synchronized in package.json, package-lock.json and UI.

## Reported visual defects
- User marked misplaced arrow strokes and dimension lines crossing timber in a two-wall California-corner 3D construction.
- Hand-drawn digit outlines were malformed/visually confusing (e.g. 6/9) and too large.
- Numeric labels must stay parallel to their own wall on the ground plane.

## Correction
1. `dimension-model.ts`: put all per-wall and total-dimension lines **outside** the measured physical timber footprint on the near negative Z/X sides; extension feet begin 100 mm beyond the actual wall assembly AABB, not at studs. Measurement `from/to` and canonical JSON never change.
2. `DimensionOverlay.ts`: replace homemade stroke glyphs with **true vector shapes** from the bundled Three.js Helvetiker typeface, glyph size 0.18 m rather than 0.36 m. No canvas, bitmaps, or mipmap blur; correct glyph counters and widths. Only numerals appear.
3. Arrows are **solid flat triangles at both ends of the dimension stroke**, same XZ ground Y as extension lines and dimension stroke. For short dimensions arrowheads shrink proportionally.
4. `SceneView.tsx`: each label is aligned along its individual dimension line, with only an optional 180° flip as the orbit camera crosses to the other side. That means it is **always parallel to its wall** and never billboarded vertically.
5. New `DimensionOverlay.test.ts`: vector font bounding geometry and no texture, flat arrow and line Y, parallel label directions on X/-Z through camera rotations, dimension lines outside construction. Existing model tests updated for near exterior side.
6. Playwright Chromium screenshots (top/isometric) and functional JSON/garage/windows regression, full CI lint/tests/build.

No modifications to walls, joints, doors, opening markers, structural data or canonical JSON. No engineering strength implied.
