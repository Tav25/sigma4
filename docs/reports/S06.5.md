## User-approved reference geometry

- World `(0,0,0)` is the **left lower start of the first wall** in the default model, not the visual centre of the camera.
- First wall `+X`, `+Y` up, wall depth along Z. A wall length 4000 spans X=0..4000 mm, never X=-2000..2000 due to camera re-centring.
- Attached walls have their own origins in canonical JSON, and their local U for doors/windows. Preserve old project coordinates on import; do NOT silently rebase users' projects.
- Dimension units: mm.

## Changes

1. `dimension-model.ts`: deterministic annotation data from canonical `building.walls` lengths and **real oriented 3D timber AABB** for overall exterior X/Z. Correctly considers California corner, rotated walls, top-plate laps, open wall segments and any offset walls; dimensions are derived only and not stored twice.
2. `DimensionOverlay.ts`: world-space dimension segments, extension lines, arrow heads and labelled billboards; dedicated marker at **world zero** and labelled +X/+Y/+Z axes. Visuals are non-selectable, independent from timber and opening handling, and disposed on updates and unmount.
3. `SceneView.tsx`: show/hide overlay, live dimension data, display of outer sizes, camera framing including annotation extremities for top and isometric views.
4. `App.tsx` + styles: checkbox 'Показывать размеры стен' and memoized detached scene projection and derived dimension model. Selection, imports, Undo, existing garage and openings remain intact.
5. `dimension-model.test.ts`: exact 4m original wall anchor +X; corner footprint; dynamic length change; nonzero imported offset preserved, nine walls and unique dimension identifiers.
6. `browser-smoke.mjs`: toggle, 2-wall corner and 9-wall project, import 4m wall, verify original origin in exported JSON, modify to 5m, capture rendered screenshots.

## Scope and limits

- Per-wall displayed length = **authored nominal wall length**, from its world-local start to end; total exterior X/Z = actual AABB of placed board meshes including joint geometry. The two are not synonymous.
- No change to canonical JSON schema, wall origin fields, California corner geometry, generator rules, opening IDs or wall connection topology.
- Exterior total sizes are bounding dimensions of the actual **timber model**, not roofing, cladding or finished surfaces.
- Existing imported projects with nonzero first-wall origins are preserved without data loss; global world axes remain stationary at (0,0,0). In new projects, first wall begins at zero.
- No new support for arbitrary rotated wall axes, T joints or strength calculations. `engineeringStatus` remains `not_evaluated`.
